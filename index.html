STRIATUM 4.0 — IGMCRI Medical Symposium 2026

STRIATUM 4.0 is the annual medical symposium of Indira Gandhi Medical College & Research Institute (IGMCRI), Puducherry, presented by SIGMA 2026. Its tagline: "A Familiar Journey, A Deeper Dive."
