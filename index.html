STRIATUM 4.0 — IGMCRI Medical Conclave 2026

STRIATUM 4.0 is the annual medical conclave of Indira Gandhi Medical College & Research Institute (IGMCRI), Puducherry, presented by SIGMA 2026. Its tagline: "A Familiar Journey, A Deeper Dive."

STRIATUM 4.0 is a medical conclave in Puducherry, India, from 28 October – 1 November 2026, with clinical workshops, quizzes, research presentations and creative events.
